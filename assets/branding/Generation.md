## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create a new logo concept for ZIP Select, a desktop ZIP extraction application. The user prefers the recognizable zipped folder with a check mark over an abstract Z monogram, but dislikes the previous detailed shaded illustrative style. Keep the recognizable IDEA, redesign its STYLE completely. ONE compact flat pictogram: clean geometric folder silhouette, a short highly simplified zipper composed of only 3 or 4 broad interlocking teeth, and one small checkmark integrated naturally into the folder face. Refined minimal editorial / wayfinding-symbol aesthetic, slightly rounded precise corners, thick solid shapes, carefully balanced negative space, memorable silhouette, legible at 24px. Absolutely flat two-dimensional solid color fills, no gradients, no highlights, no shadows, no gloss, no material texture, no 3D, no perspective, no realistic zipper pull, no tiny detail. A single medium blue folder and off-white/light blue zipper and check details, limited to 2 or 3 flat colors. No text, no letters, no surrounding app tile, no presentation board, no mockup, no other objects, no trademark references. Single centered symbol, genuinely transparent background, square canvas 1024x1024 with generous even padding. The result should feel like a crisply drawn professional software pictogram, not a rendered folder illustration.
